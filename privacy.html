Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

BlazeWise is an app for fire risk assessors (Android and Windows). This page explains what information it uses and where that information goes. In short: your work stays on your own devices and in your own Google Drive. We don't sell or share it, and we don't run a server that stores it.

Information kept on your device

Everything you put into BlazeWise (clients, properties, jobs, assessments, findings, photos, voice notes, floor plans, reports, invoices and quotes) is saved in a database and folder on your own phone or PC. Voice-to-text and the app's suggestions run on the device itself; no recordings or text are sent anywhere for that.

Your BlazeWise account

To use the app, a business owner creates an account with their name, business name, email address and phone number. These details, and a scrambled password check, are kept in a private Google Sheet run by the BlazeWise developer, so that the account can be approved, its access period shown, workers added, and forgotten passwords reset (a 6-digit code is emailed). Workers who join a business with an invite code are listed in the same way. Nothing else from the app is sent to the developer.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose to connect Google, BlazeWise uses Google's sign-in to:

  • Google Drive – keep your phone, PC and team in step, and share a single job with another assessor. BlazeWise only works with the files and folders it created or that you chose to open with it. Copies of your jobs, photos and reports are stored in your Google Drive.
  • Google Calendar – put your visits into a "BlazeWise" calendar in your own Google account.
  • Your email address – to show which Google account is connected.

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your device and are used only to talk to Google on your behalf. You can disconnect at any time in Settings › Connections, which also revokes BlazeWise's access. BlazeWise's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never read by people except when you ask for help and show it yourself.

Sending and sharing

When you send a report, invoice or photo, BlazeWise opens your own email program or your phone's share menu; it doesn't send anything itself. When you share a job for editing, only that job (with its photos) is placed in a Google Drive folder shared with the email address you choose; your other jobs, prices, invoices and bank details are never included.

Updates

BlazeWise may check this website for a newer version. That check sends no personal information.

Keeping and deleting information

Your work stays until you delete it from the app or remove the app. You can back it up and delete it in Settings › Data. To close your account and remove your details from the account list, email us.

Children

BlazeWise is a professional tool and is not meant for children.
