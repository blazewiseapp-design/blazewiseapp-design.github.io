BlazeWise

Fire risk assessments on your phone and PC.

BlazeWise keeps everything for a visit inside one job: the client and property, the calendar visit, the assessment with photos and voice notes, findings and their actions, floor plans, a one-click PDF report with every link already in place, and the invoice.

  • Works offline. Your work is saved on your own device first.
  • Talk-to-text that runs on the device, with fire-safety words.
  • Reports in your own branding, with the action plan linked to every finding.
  • Phone, PC and your team kept in step through your own Google Drive (optional).

BlazeWise is available to approved businesses. For an account, email blazewiseapp@gmail.com.
